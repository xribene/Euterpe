# Synth

A polyphonic synthesizer: play the keyboard (laptop, on-screen or MIDI) and the agent generates the sound, in
the browser, through Euterpe's audio output.

## Settings

- **Waveform**: sine, triangle, saw or square.
- **Cutoff** and **Resonance**: the low-pass filter.
- **Attack**, **Decay**, **Sustain**, **Release**: the envelope of every note.
