# Copycat

I copy what you play on a 16th-note grid: the same notes, a few 16ths later and shifted in pitch.

## Settings

- **Randomness**: 0 copies you exactly; higher values add random semitones.
- **Delay**: how many 16th notes later I answer (at least 1).
- **Pitch Shift**: semitones between your note and mine (-12: an octave lower).
